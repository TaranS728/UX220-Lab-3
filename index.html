The Best Movie I've Seen Lately

Guardians of the Galaxy

The best movie that I've seen lately is Guardians of the Galaxy. The mix of action sequences, comedy, and emotional moments is why I enjoyed it as much as I did, I'd be laughing one moment and then tearing up the next. The movie follows a group of characters in Space who come across each other through suboptimal circumstances, and despite all their differences, must work together as a team to achieve their goals.

My Favourite Part

One of my favourite things about the movie is the group of characters themselves, Star-Lord, Gamora, Drax, Rocket, and Groot. They all have different personalities which makes their interactions all that more entertaining, especially Rocket and Groot, whos friendship added a lot more humour and heart to the movie.

Why I Would Recommend It

I would recommend Guardians of the Galacy because it doesn't feel like an average superhero movie. The classic soundtrack, filled with music from the 60's and 70's, the humour, the lovable characters, and the adventerous space setting gives the movie it's own identity and personality. It's a movie that you can watch again and again and still enjoy.
